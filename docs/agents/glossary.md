## Terminology

**Activity** — A real-world outing recorded by a GPS device, such as a hike,
run, or ride.

**Track** or **tracklog** — The ordered GPS observations recorded during an
activity and imported from a GPX file.

**Route** — A shareable record of an activity. It contains canonical route
data, GPX-derived information, user-managed route information, visibility, and
optional route photos.

**Recorded point** — A coordinate from the source track, optionally including
elevation and an absolute timestamp.

**Track segment** — An ordered, continuous portion of a track. A segment
boundary indicates a recording discontinuity and does not prove that the gap
was traversed.

**Canonical route data** — The provider-independent, authoritative record of
the route's recorded observations. Other representations and route-domain
behavior are derived from it.

**GPX-derived information** — Route information calculated from the imported
track and not editable by the route owner, such as activity date, distance,
duration, average pace, elevation gain, recorded-point count, and geometry.

**User-managed route information** — Route information the owner may edit
independently of the recorded track: title, activity type, notes, and
visibility.

**Route owner** — The authenticated user associated with a route who may edit
or delete it and manage its photos.

**Public route** — A route readable by anyone but editable only by its owner.

**Private route** — A route readable and editable only by its owner.

**Route photo** — An image associated with a route. Its taken time, recorded
location, and upload time are distinct information.
